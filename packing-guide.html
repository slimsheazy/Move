Before You Start Anything
Get your supplies first — one trip, today.
Boxes: Ask on Nextdoor or Facebook Marketplace for free ones first. Liquor stores always have sturdy small boxes. Buy the rest at U-Haul or Home Depot.
What to buy: Tape gun + 4 rolls of tape  ·  Black permanent marker (2)  ·  Packing paper or newspaper  ·  2–3 wardrobe boxes for hanging clothes  ·  Bubble wrap for fragile stuff  ·  Large zip-lock bags for small hardware and parts  ·  Labels or blue tape for marking boxes
Box types to have on hand: Small boxes for books + heavy things  ·  Medium boxes for most things  ·  Large boxes for light bulky stuff only (pillows, linens, lampshades)
